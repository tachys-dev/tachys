# Citation

If you use tachys in your research, please cite it:

```bibtex
@software{tachys,
  author = {Rende, Riccardo and Viteritti, Luciano Loris},
  title  = {tachys: Variational Monte Carlo for quantum many-body systems},
  url    = {https://github.com/tachys-dev/tachys},
  year   = {2026},
}
```

If you use one of the following, please also cite the corresponding papers:

- The `SR` optimizer (stochastic reconfiguration):
  [Rende et al., *Commun. Phys.* **7**, 260 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-024-01732-4)
  and
  [Chen & Heyl, *Nat. Phys.* **20**, 1476 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-024-02566-1).
- The `SPRING` optimizer:
  [Goldshlager, Abrahamsen & Lin, *J. Comput. Phys.* **516**, 113351 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2024.113351).
  The `MARCH` optimizer:
  [Gu et al., *Nat. Commun.* **17**, 7838 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-74028-6).
- The Vision Transformer wave function, `SpinViT`:
  [Viteritti, Rende & Becca, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **130**, 236401 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.236401)
  and
  [Viteritti et al., *Phys. Rev. B* **111**, 134411 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.111.134411);
  with spatial attention, also
  [Viteritti et al., arXiv:2602.02665 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02665).
- The transformer backflow determinant, `FermionicTransformer`:
  [Gu et al., *Nat. Commun.* **17**, 7838 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-74028-6).
- The symmetry-preserving backflow pairing (SBP) wave function:
  [Rende, Viteritti & Georges, arXiv:2608.12465 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12465).
- Foundation neural-network quantum states:
  [Rende et al., *Nat. Commun.* **16**, 7213 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62098-x).
